# Privacy policy

- Read `PRIVACY.md` before changes affecting its disclosures, including dependency changes that alter data handling.
- Check file access and processing, collection and sharing, local storage paths and contents, retention and deletion, external applications and services, accounts, advertising, analytics, telemetry, and support contacts.
- Update affected disclosures in the same change when actual behavior changes. Verify claims against code and configuration; do not promise unsupported privacy guarantees.
- Keep wording concise and user-facing. Preserve warnings about public reports and explain deletion steps and their effects accurately.
- Leave `PRIVACY.md` unchanged when disclosures remain accurate. No routine date bumps, speculative disclosures, or duplicate policy files.
- Final summary: note privacy-related policy updates, or why no update was needed when this skill applies.
