# DualCommander Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the DualCommander desktop application.

## File access

DualCommander accesses file names, folder paths, metadata, and file contents as
needed to browse folders and archives, search, show previews, and perform file
operations you request. These files may contain personal information. Processing
takes place on your device, with access to network-mounted or synced folders
handled by your operating system and the services you use.

## Collection and sharing

DualCommander does not send your files or personal information to its developers.
It has no advertising, analytics, or telemetry, and does not sell personal
information. No account is required to use the app.

## Local storage and retention

DualCommander saves preferences, session state, and favourite folder paths in
`.dualcommander` within your home folder, including `settings.json`, `state.json`,
and `favourites.json`. Saved information can include visited folder paths, window
settings, and external application commands.

This information remains on your device until you change or remove it. To remove
these saved files, close DualCommander and delete the `.dualcommander` folder.
This resets saved preferences, session state, and favourites; it does not delete
the files you manage with the app. Uninstalling the app may leave this folder
behind.

## External applications and services

When you copy files, DualCommander places their paths on the system clipboard
where supported, so other applications can paste them. Pasting files reads file
references from that clipboard. Clipboard managers and operating-system clipboard
sync may retain or share these paths under their own policies. Cut remains local
to DualCommander; external cut selections are pasted as copies.

When you open a file in another application, launch an external tool, or follow
a website link, the selected application or service receives the information
needed for that action, such as a file path or website address. Its own privacy
policy applies. Network storage, cloud sync providers, operating systems, and
app stores may process information independently under their own policies.

## Support and privacy questions

Contact the project through
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/dualcommander/dualcommander/issues).
Information you submit there is handled by GitHub under
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
Public issues are visible to others. Do not include sensitive files, private
paths, credentials, or other personal information in public reports.
